I used to be like everyone else, seeing (Neo)Vim as the ultimate code editor for programmers and a ticket to the geek community. But after reading the guides, a rebellious ghost idea starts to linger in my head: maybe its actual fate is a prose editor for LaTeX and Markdown. After four years of first-hand experiences, I am even more confident to suggest every LaTeX and Markdown writers try (Neo)Vim for prose writing.
